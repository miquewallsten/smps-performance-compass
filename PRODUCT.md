# Product

## Register

product

## Users

HR managers, supervisors, and employees at SMPS, a legal and administrative services firm in Mexico. Supervisors use the system to evaluate their team members and track completion. Employees use it for self-evaluations, action plans, and personal objectives. HR managers oversee the entire evaluation cycle, configure periods, manage users, and generate reports. Most users are busy professionals who need to complete evaluations quickly and accurately — not explore a UI.

## Product Purpose

SMPS Performance Compass is an internal performance evaluation platform that manages annual employee assessment cycles: self-evaluations, supervisor evaluations, action plans, personal objectives, and org-wide reporting. It supports role-based views (employee, supervisor, admin, superuser), period-based evaluation cycles, vacation requests, internal communications, and a copilot assistant. Success means evaluations get completed on time, results are clear, and managers spend minimal time figuring out the tool.

## Brand Personality

Authoritative, sleek, modern. Corporate confidence without the cliché. More motion, less padding — the interface should feel efficient and decisive, not cozy or casual. Three-word personality: **sleek, decisive, authoritative**.

## Anti-references

- Not playful, gamified, or kindergarten-like — no bright pastels, emojis, or whimsical illustrations.
- Not a typical SaaS dashboard — no generic purple gradients, rounded pill buttons, or "startup hero" aesthetics.
- Not Mickey Mouse — nothing cartoonish, juvenile, or overly friendly.

## Design Principles

1. **Decisive density** — Use space efficiently. Less padding, tighter layouts. Information density should feel confident, not cluttered.
2. **Purposeful motion** — Transitions and animations should communicate state changes clearly, not decorate. Micro-interactions that feel fast and responsive.
3. **Corporate restraint** — The navy/red/gold palette is authoritative. Let it breathe with stark contrast, not decorative noise.
4. **Expert tool feel** — The interface should feel like a precision instrument, not a consumer app. Data and actions front and center.
5. **No SaaS clichés** — Avoid default shadcn spacing, generic card grids, and "friendly" empty states. This is a professional evaluation tool, not a project management app.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No specific accessibility requirements mandated. Standard contrast and readability practices apply where they support the sleek, professional aesthetic.
